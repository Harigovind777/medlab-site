What we do

A distributor, not a middleman.

Manufacturers build the implant. Hospitals need it in theatre, in the right size, on the right day. Medlab Enterprises is the link that holds — dealership, stock and delivery under one roof in Thiruvananthapuram.

Dealership

We hold the authorised dealership for AOSYS orthopedic implants. Every item we supply comes through the manufacturer's own channel — no parallel imports, no repacked stock, full traceability on the batch.

Stock & sizes

Implants fail the surgeon when the size isn't there. We carry depth across the size range rather than one of everything, so the plate length and screw diameter you asked for is the one that arrives.

Delivery

Orders confirmed by phone are picked and dispatched from our Nemom premises. Tell us the urgency when you call and we'll tell you what is realistic.

Product range

Nine lines, from trauma plating to power tools.

The full AOSYS portfolio, available to hospitals and surgeons across our four districts. Every implant is offered in surgical stainless steel and titanium.

01 / TRAUMA

Trauma plating

Locking and compression plates for the distal radius, radial head, wrist fusion and the long bones.

 02 / NAILS

Interlocking nails

Intramedullary nails for femoral, tibial and humeral shaft fractures, with locking screws and end caps.

 03 / SPINE

Spine systems

Pedicle screws, MIS fenestrated systems, anterior and posterior cervical plating, PEEK interbody cages.

 04 / ARTHRO

Arthroscopy system

Camera, light source, shaver and the instrument set for knee and shoulder arthroscopic procedures.

 05 / MAXFAX

Maxillofacial system

Low-profile mini and micro plating for midface, orbital and mandibular fracture fixation.

 06 / RECON

Mandibular reconstruction

Heavy reconstruction plates and condylar prostheses for segmental mandibular defects.

 07 / EXFIX

External fixators

Rail, ring and tubular fixator systems with Schanz pins, clamps and connecting rods.

 08 / POWER

Power tools

Battery and pneumatic drills, reamers, oscillating saws and the sterilisation cases they ship in.

 09 / FOOT

Foot & ankle

Anatomic plating for the distal fibula, calcaneus and midfoot, plus compression and cannulated screws.
