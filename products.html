02 / NAILS

Interlocking nails

Intramedullary nailing for diaphyseal fractures of the long bones — cannulated, statically and dynamically lockable, with the jig and instrument set to place them.

Nails are supplied with matched locking bolts and end caps. Diameter and length are confirmed on the order — tell us the bone, the side and the measurement you templated.

  • Femoral interlocking nail — antegrade and retrograde
  • Proximal femoral nail for intertrochanteric fractures
  • Tibial interlocking nail, cannulated
  • Humeral interlocking nail
  • Locking bolts, end caps and poller screws
  • Targeting jig and reaming instrument sets

Arthroscopy system

A complete arthroscopic tower plus the hand instruments, for knee and shoulder procedures in theatres setting up minimally invasive joint surgery.

Supplied as a system or by component. If you are commissioning a new arthroscopy theatre, call us before you order — the useful conversation is about what you already have.

05 / MAXFAX

Maxillofacial system

Mini and micro plating for the facial skeleton, where profile height matters as much as fixation strength because the plate sits directly under thin tissue.

Predominantly supplied in titanium. Plates are bendable in theatre to the contour of the fracture.

  • Straight, L, Y, T and X mini plates
  • Micro plating for orbital rim and nasal fractures
  • Orbital floor mesh and sheets
  • Midface and zygomatic fixation plates
  • Self-tapping screws from 1.5 mm to 2.4 mm
  • Bending, cutting and screwdriving instruments
06 / RECON

Mandibular reconstruction plating

Heavy-section reconstruction plates for segmental mandibular defects following tumour resection, gunshot injury or comminuted fracture.

These are load-bearing constructs, not load-sharing ones. Locking versions maintain the reconstructed contour without relying on plate-to-bone compression.

  • Mandibular reconstruction plates, locking and non-locking
  • Pre-bent angled and condylar plates
  • Condylar head prostheses
  • 2.4 mm and 2.7 mm locking screws
  • Heavy bending irons and plate-holding forceps
07 / EXFIX

External fixators

Temporary and definitive external fixation for open fractures, damage control orthopedics, deformity correction and limb lengthening.

Frames are modular. We supply full sets to trauma centres and individual components to units that already hold a frame inventory.

  • Tubular fixator — rods, clamps and pin-to-bar connectors
  • Rail fixator for lengthening and transport
  • Ring fixator with rings, rods, wires and tensioners
  • Schanz screws and half pins across diameters
  • Wrist, ankle and pelvic frame configurations
  • Pin insertion instruments and wire cutters

Includes the fusion and osteotomy hardware for elective forefoot correction as well as the trauma range.

Every line, three grades

Say the grade when you order.

Material changes the price, the imaging behaviour and the fatigue life. We quote it explicitly rather than assuming a default. All implants are manufactured by AOSYS under drug manufacturing licence MFG/MD/2022/000248.

SS316L

Surgical stainless steel

ASTM F138. The general-purpose grade for trauma plating and screws. Strong, well-understood, and the most economical of the three.

SS316LVM

Vacuum-melted steel

Remelted under vacuum for lower inclusion content. Chosen where fatigue performance and corrosion resistance need to be better than standard 316L.

Ti-6Al-4V ELI

Titanium alloy

Lower modulus, excellent biocompatibility, less imaging artefact. The usual choice for spine, maxillofacial and any implant expected to stay in long term.
