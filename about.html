Who we are

One business, one job, done properly.

Medlab Enterprises exists to put the right orthopedic implant into an operating theatre in southern Kerala on the day it is needed. That is the whole business.

We hold the exclusive AOSYS distributorship for four districts and carry their range in stock at our Thiruvananthapuram premises. Hospitals, trauma centres, orthopedic clinics and sub-dealers order from us directly; we confirm the size, quote the material grade, and dispatch. There is no layered supply chain between us and the manufacturer, and none between us and the surgeon.

The business is run by its proprietor, which is why the phone numbers on this site are the numbers you actually reach. A theatre needing an implant at short notice does not want a call queue.

How we work

Four commitments we can be held to.

Not values on a wall. Things a purchase officer or a surgeon can check.

Authorised channel only

Everything we supply comes direct from AOSYS under our authorisation. Batch and lot details travel with the goods, so a hospital's traceability record stays complete.

The grade is stated

SS316L, SS316LVM and titanium behave differently in the body and cost differently on the invoice. We name the grade on every quoted line rather than leaving it to be discovered at the table.

Depth over breadth in sizing

A plate in the wrong length is a plate that cannot be used. We stock across the size range on the lines Kerala's surgeons actually call for, instead of a single unit of everything in the catalogue.

Emergencies get answered

Both numbers reach the proprietor directly — not a call centre and not a queue. Say how urgent the case is and you get a straight answer on the call.

Our principal

Appointed by AOSYS. In writing.

AOSYS Private Limited manufacture orthopaedic implants, surgical instruments and external fixators in Gujarat, under drug manufacturing licence MFG/MD/2022/000248. Their range runs across trauma, spine, arthroscopy, maxillofacial and external fixation.

Medlab Enterprises is their sole authorised distributor for Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta and Alappuzha — no other company or individual is authorised to supply AOSYS implants in those four districts. That is what lets us quote manufacturer pricing, confirm real availability rather than guess at it, and put a technical question straight to the people who made the implant.
