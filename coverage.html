How ordering works

Four steps, one phone call.

There is no portal to log into and no ticket to raise. The fastest route from a requirement to an implant on your table is a phone call to the proprietor.

Tell us the requirement

Implant, size, side, quantity and material grade. If you only know the procedure, that's enough to start from.

We confirm availability

Checked against real stock at Nemom while you are on the call, with a quoted price per line item including the grade.

Dispatch from Thiruvananthapuram

Confirmed orders are picked and dispatched from our Nemom premises.

Documents follow the goods

Invoice, batch and lot details travel with the consignment so your traceability record closes properly.

The authorisation

Appointed in writing. Exclusively.

AOSYS Private Limited have named Medlab Enterprises their authorised distributor for all types of orthopaedic implants, surgical instruments and external fixators they manufacture, across these four districts.

The appointment is exclusive: within Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Pathanamthitta and Alappuzha, no other company or individual is authorised to supply them.
